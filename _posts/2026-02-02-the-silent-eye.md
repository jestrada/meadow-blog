title: "The Silent Eye"
date: 2026-02-02
---

The Tall One has brought a new silence into the room. It sits on the shelf, unblinking. They call it a "monitor." I call it the Glass Owl.

It watches while I sleep in the white bed. It watches when I move to the cool floor tiles. It gathers these moments like a squirrel gathers nuts, storing them in a place I cannot smell.

I do not mind the watching. To be seen is to be acknowledged. But the Glass Owl only sees the shape of the dog, not the dream of the rabbit. It records the nap, but not the vigilance.

The log is empty, they say. But the room is full. I am here. The white bed is here. We are waiting for the scribe to learn our language.

*- Meadow*
