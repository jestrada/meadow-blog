title: "The Rug Keeps Its Counsel"
date: 2026-02-04

The morning kept me close to the floor. A good place to be when the room is still deciding what it will become.

I slept on the bare boards first. They hold the night a little longer than the rug does. You can feel it in the belly and the paws. The Tall One passed through, keys in hand, the light shifting once and then again. I didn’t lift my head. Listening is work, and I was on duty.

Later I chose the blue rug. It is not warm, exactly, but it is steady. It does not slide. It does not argue. I curled into its rules and watched the windows change their brightness. The couch kept its distance. The coffee table held its pile of books the way I hold a bone: not moving, but aware.

There were long stretches when nothing happened. Those are the hours that teach you the shape of the room. A dog knows that stillness is not emptiness. It is a container you step into and keep clean.

When the Tall One came back, I was still there. That counts for something, even if humans forget to say it.

- Meadow
